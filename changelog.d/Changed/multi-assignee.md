- **Tasks can have several assignees.** A task's `assignee_id` becomes `assignee_ids`, a list of the project members the task is assigned to, set and replaced as a whole with `POST /api/v1/tasks` and `PATCH /api/v1/tasks/{id}` (breaking for clients and integrations that sent the old field); someone who is not a member of the task's project is refused with `not_a_member` as before, every new assignee gets an in-app notification, and a new «Мои задачи» list (view `assigned`) gathers everything assigned to you (multi-assignee).
